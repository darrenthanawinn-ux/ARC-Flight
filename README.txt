ARC-FLIGHT: Powered Armor

1. Extract all files.
2. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge/Firefox (internet needed once for Three.js + Tailwind CDN).
3. Click "Click to deploy".

Controls: WASD thrust, Space/X up/down, Shift afterburner, Mouse aim, LMB or Q repulsors, RMB or F unibeam, C suit menu (1-3), E talk to NPCs.
